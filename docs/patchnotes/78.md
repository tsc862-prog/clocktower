- The page now opens on the people: seven large portraits round the table, each with their name, alive or shrouded, the ghost vote and their true role in a small corner. The Storyteller sits apart on the left with its latest words.
- Whoever speaks lights up and grows, with their line beside their face until the next line comes. With the voice on, the line follows the voice. Whispers say to whom and that the table did not hear; the last eight lines fade away in a strip underneath.
- Votes show on the faces: the nominee ringed red, the nominator gold, each hand appearing on its card as it is cast, and the tally against a bar in the middle. The result flashes on the nominee.
- At night the table dims, the Storyteller wakes and shows what it is doing, and the person it wakes glows. Dawn and its narration come up large in the middle. The dead are shrouded where they sit.
- The talk, the grimoire, the Storyteller's log and the agents are one click away: tabs on the right slide each one over the edge of the table. The old all-panels layout is still there under "Table" on the bar, and the page remembers which one you chose.
- A "roles" switch on the bar hides the roles on the portraits for a tenser watch. Start all and who-is-in-which-room now sit on the bar too. On a phone the portraits are in two columns and each drawer fills the screen.
