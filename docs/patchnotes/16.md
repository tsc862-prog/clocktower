---
title: "A real game now sits behind the seats"
date: 2026-11-07
area: rules
issues: [1]
---
- The relay can now host a real game (pick it with a game number), so a seat that joins sees its own role, what it was told at night and what was said at the table, and nothing else.
- The day has a shape: three rounds of talk where each player speaks or passes once, then nominations, one nomination per player, then the votes, one hand at a time.
- Whispers reach only the two players; everyone else just sees that a whisper happened.
- Every call the Storyteller makes, including closing a vote or ending the day, is kept with its reason.
- If no Storyteller agent is present, a stand-in Storyteller can make its calls so the players can still play.
