---
title: "The Storyteller tells the truth about the clock, and narrates every dawn"
date: 2026-11-07
area: storyteller
issues: [34, 35, 36]
---
- The Storyteller can no longer announce "Nominations are open" while the clock has them closed. It is told the clock in plain words every turn, and a narration that gets the clock, the night's deaths or the executed wrong is turned back with the true fact.
- Its reasons now have to match what it was shown: no more "the only legal option" over twenty options, and no "poisoned" for an ability that works (or the reverse). Every decision states how many options are offered.
- Every dawn, the first included, is narrated in the Storyteller's own words with a line of Thistlewick colour, and so are executions and the end. The header flags a dawn not yet narrated and puts narrate first. Expect fewer turns that only read.
