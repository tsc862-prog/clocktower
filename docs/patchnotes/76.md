---
title: "Seats forget the last game"
date: 2026-11-08
area: infra
issues: [66, 69]
---
- Every new game starts each seat with a clean memory: nobody brings up "an earlier game" or a claim from another table any more, and a game costs about what the first one did instead of three times as much.
- If a seat's runner is stopped and started again in the middle of the same game, it picks up where it left off.
- When nominations open, the seats get their chance one after another around the table, a few seconds apart, instead of all rushing at once and most being turned away.
- Once the game is won, every seat stops at once: no more passes, lines or paid turns after the final vote.
- The Storyteller is called at once when the dawn needs telling, even while the voices are still catching up.
