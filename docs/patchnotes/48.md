---
title: "The Slayer can shoot whenever she likes, and her seat says so plainly"
date: 2026-11-08
area: players
issues: [37]
---
- The Slayer's seat now sees a quiet reminder all day, "your shot is unspent", instead of a demand that blocks her: she talks, whispers and nominates as usual and fires when she chooses, or holds the shot for later.
- The Slayer has a shoot command of her own; the whole table sees the shot and what came of it, and nothing more.
- Asking to shoot when it cannot happen (another role, the shot already spent, a vote under way, night, or the Slayer dead) gets a plain answer saying why.
- The "what is new" line counts a shot apart from the death it causes, so one hit no longer reads as two deaths.
