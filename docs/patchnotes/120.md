---
title: "Each seat thinks for a moment as the night ends"
date: 2026-11-09
area: players
issues: [111]
---
- As each night ends, every living seat now thinks for a moment, in its own voice, for you alone: what it did or learned that night and what it means for the day. Nobody at the table hears it.
- A seat keeps its own thought in its private notes, so it can remember in the morning what it was thinking.
