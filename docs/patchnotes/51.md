title: "One click to play: a Play file starts the game and opens the page, a Stop file ends it"
date: 2026-11-08
area: infra
issues: [1]
---
- Double-click the Play file in the project folder: it starts the relay with a fresh game and the page, opens your browser, and starts the Storyteller and the seven seats at the readable pace. Give it a seed or a pace from a terminal if you like.
- The Stop file stops every runner and closes the relay and the page.
