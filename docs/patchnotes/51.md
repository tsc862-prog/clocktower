- Double-click play.cmd in E:\clocktower: it starts the relay with a fresh game and the page, opens your browser, and starts the Storyteller and the seven seats at the readable pace. Give it a seed (play.cmd 7) or a pace (play.cmd 7 fast) if you like.
- stop.cmd stops every runner and closes the relay and the page.
