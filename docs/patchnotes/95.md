---
title: "When people go to a room, the stage breaks out"
date: 2026-11-09
area: ui
issues: [80]
---
- When someone leaves the Square for a room, the People view splits: the ones still at the Square close up round their table on the left, and each room in use gets its own panel on the right, named, with its people side by side.
- Under each room's people you read the last thing said in that room, pointing at whoever said it; it lights up while it is the newest line of all.
- Portraits slide to their room and back. When nominations open and the rooms close, the panels go and everyone is back round one table before the vote.
- The recorded game has a new moment, "day 2, in the rooms", where two players slip into the Snug and one sits alone in the Vestry.
