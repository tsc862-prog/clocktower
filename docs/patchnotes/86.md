- The portraits and the table now size themselves to the window you watch in, from a laptop screen to the full 3440 wide: no face is cut off at the top, and no two portraits sit on each other.
- The dawn, the vote tally, the night and the winner stay inside the middle of the table and shrink to fit it; a long narration scrolls inside the middle instead of running over the faces.
- The Storyteller's words on the left and the strip of recent lines underneath stay fully in view. The strip shows as many lines as the window has room for.
- On a wide window the seats spread along the long sides of the table rather than bunching at the ends.
