---
title: The players can use the rooms
date: 2026-11-08
area: players
issues: [28]
---
- The players can now see who is where, go to a room, take someone from the Square along, and come back; moving costs them nothing.
- Each player's turn now starts with where they are, who is with them, who is away in which room, how many places each room has free, and who went off together since they last looked.
- When a player speaks, they are told who heard it: the whole table, the people in their room, or nobody if they are alone there.
- A player who tries to whisper to someone who is not beside them at the table is told the two names they can whisper to, and that anyone else needs a room.
- The players are told that rooms are for making a plan with a few people, that the table sees them go, and that the rooms close when nominations open.
