---
title: "Players check a claim before they repeat it, take each other aside, and notice when their notes are out of date"
date: 2026-11-08
area: players
issues: [67, 68, 71]
---
- A player can now look up every role each player has claimed out loud, with the day and the exact words, and is asked to do so before repeating "she changed her claim"; such an accusation has to quote both lines. In game 3 a good Ravenkeeper was executed on a claim she never made.
- Each player also sees a short list of the claims made so far at the top of every turn.
- When a player has a reason to talk in private (an evil team-mate, someone who just questioned or accused them, a player they doubt, someone claiming their own role), they are told whom to take to which room and that it costs nothing; the old advice that put them off rooms is gone. Expect to see players slip into the Snug, the Vestry or the Taproom.
- A player whose private notes have fallen behind the game ("Thea is dead" while she lives, an old role after becoming the Imp) is told which note is out of date. A Scarlet Woman who becomes the Imp is told plainly what changed and since when.
- At four players or fewer, the "nominate anyway" prompt no longer contradicts the stakes count.
