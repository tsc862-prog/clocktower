---
title: "The Storyteller greets each dawn with one touch of the town"
date: 2026-11-08
area: storyteller
issues: [70]
---
- Every dawn the Storyteller now says two sentences: who died in the night (or that nobody did), then one small thing about Thistlewick, like fog on Lamp Row or the heron on the millpond. No touch comes twice in one game, and a different game starts with different ones.
- A death gets a few words about the neighbour who died, not just the name.
- An execution gets its own line at dusk, as soon as it happens, instead of waiting for the next morning; the end of the game gets one line too.
- The Storyteller is told to speak first at dawn, before the table starts talking, and to stop spending turns only reading when nothing is waiting on it.
