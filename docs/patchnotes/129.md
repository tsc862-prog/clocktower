---
title: "The Storyteller tells an execution with its true count"
date: 2026-11-09
area: storyteller
issues: [124]
---
- When a vote puts someone to death, the Storyteller now gives that vote's own count against the number needed, not the count of a later vote.
- When a second nomination also went to a vote and fell short, the Storyteller says so in a few words of its own ("drew three, one short"), and never folds the two votes into one.
- A narration that gives the wrong count is turned back before you hear it, and the Storyteller is told the true one.
