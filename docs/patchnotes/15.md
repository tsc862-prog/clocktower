title: "The Storyteller gets its tools"
date: 2026-11-07
area: storyteller
issues: [1]
---
- Nothing to watch yet: the Storyteller agent now has everything it needs to run a game: the grimoire, tonight's order, the decision waiting on it with the legal answers and the truth, and what was said at the table.
- Every call it makes (who the Drunk thinks they are, what the Empath learns, where a kill on the Mayor lands) carries a short reason you will be able to read; no player ever sees them.
- It can only pick answers the rules allow, and it never hears the words of a whisper, only that one happened.
- It narrates the dawn, the votes and the end in a plain, dry voice, and while the game runs it may not name anyone's role.
