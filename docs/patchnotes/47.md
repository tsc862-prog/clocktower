- The pace you pick on the game bar (fast, readable, slow) now reaches every seat when it starts, so "readable" really is about a minute between a seat's turns.
- You can change the pace of seats that are already playing, and they pick it up at their next turn.
- A pace outside what makes sense is refused with a plain message.
