- The town is called Thistlewick, and the first story is The Stopped Hour.
- All 22 roles now have names of their own, from the Gossip to the Thirteenth Stroke.
- Seven neighbours sit at the table: Odette, Bram, Ines, Corwin, Thea, Fitch and Yusuf.
- Nothing plays yet; this is the cast list the game will be built from.
