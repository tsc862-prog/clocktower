---
title: The square shows who is in which room
date: 2026-11-08
area: ui
issues: [28]
---
- While the rooms are open, anyone who has left the Square has a small gold tag under their seat naming the room they are in. Their seat is dimmed while they are away.
- The three rooms (the Snug, the Vestry, the Taproom) sit under the table as small round discs, with the portrait or initial of everyone inside and how full each room is.
- One line under the table says who is where, for example "Odette and Bram in the Snug; the Vestry empty".
- When nominations open and the rooms close, the tags, the discs and the line go away.
