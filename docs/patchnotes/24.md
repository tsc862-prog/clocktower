- The page now has the whole table: the Square with seven seats in a circle, hands going up and down as a vote is cast and the tally against what is needed, and under each seat the role only the Storyteller (and you) can see.
- The day's talk reads in order: every line with its speaker, the Storyteller's narration, nominations, votes and executions, and whispers marked "leaned in", with the words the table did not hear.
- The grimoire, the demon's bluffs and what the game waits on sit beside a Storyteller's log that gives every call it made and why. Calls made by the scripted fallback are flagged.
- An Agents panel lists the Storyteller and each seat: what is playing it, what it has cost of its budget and its last turn. Start, stop, start all, stop all, and a Resume button when the game is paused.
- The talk stays where you are reading when you scroll up, and one click follows the newest line again.
- The relay does not send the game to the page yet, so for now you can watch a recorded game (add ?fixture to the address). New game is greyed out until the relay can start one.
