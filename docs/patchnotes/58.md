---
title: "Hear the game: each line spoken in its own voice"
date: 2026-11-08
area: ui
issues: [50]
---
- A Voice button on the game bar: click it once and the page reads each new line aloud as it arrives, each seat in its own voice and the Storyteller narrating, nominations, votes and executions included.
- The seat speaking lights up in the square while its line plays; the middle of the table lights for the Storyteller.
- Skip ends the line playing; Mute hushes everything until you unmute. Whispers are only read if you tick "whispers".
- It never falls far behind: when lines pile up, the oldest table talk is dropped first.
- It starts off, and your browser remembers your choice. If the voice engine is not running, the button says so once and stays off.
- In the talk, a line said in one of the rooms now shows where it was said ("in the nook: ..."), and each move between the Square and the rooms is a short line of its own. Room lines are read aloud too.
