---
title: "Players stop hanging the quiet roles on day 1, poison with a reason and read the log right"
date: 2026-11-09
area: players
issues: [87, 88, 89, 92]
---
- On the first day, players now know which roles have nothing to report yet (the Monk, the Mayor, the Virgin and others), so a Monk who cannot name a target should no longer be hanged for "dodging".
- The Poisoner now sees at night whose claimed ability still matters and whose is used up (a Virgin whose trap fired, a first-night role), and is told never to poison the player the demon is about to kill.
- Nominations now read "Ines (nominator) nominated Odette (nominee)", a Virgin's trap says plainly that the nominator died, and each player's turn keeps a short record of who died by day and how.
- Players count a role described in other words ("my trap fired", "I guard one player a night") as a claim, and the list of claims goes role by role, so you should stop hearing "the only Mayor left".
- Players with a count (the Chef, the Empath) are reminded that a number names nobody, and everyone is told to check what they say about the log against the log.
