---
title: Game 1 fixes for the rules
date: 2026-11-08
area: rules
issues: [30, 29, 37]
---
- Nobody loses their first say of the day any more: a pass made while it is still night no longer counts as a turn once morning comes, so every player gets a real chance to speak in the first round.
- Players who have already spoken this round are left alone until the next round, instead of being woken to try again and be turned away. Fewer wasted turns, and no lost lines.
- The Slayer can now fire the shot at any moment of the day, after hearing the talk or in the middle of the nominations, instead of having to decide at dawn before anyone has spoken. The shot still lands at once, in front of everyone.
