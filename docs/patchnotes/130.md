---
title: "The day reads better: no talking to the walls, no question asked four times"
date: 2026-11-09
area: players
issues: [122, 123, 126]
---
- A seat alone in a room no longer talks to the walls: it is told nobody is there and steps back to the Square to speak, without losing its turn.
- A seat that has had its turn is told where it spent it, in which room and to whom, so it stops trying again and reads instead.
- A seat that says to one room what it meant for another is told who did not hear it.
- Nobody asks Corwin a fourth time: seats are told to read what a player already answered before asking, and a seat asked again sees its own earlier words and answers in one line. The same line is no longer said twice, once in a room and once at the Square.
- A nomination should come with a reason that fits the seat named: seats are told a reason that fits anyone is no reason, to say what changed when they turn on someone, and to check what a ghost says happened. Who shared a room with whom, each day, can now be looked up.
