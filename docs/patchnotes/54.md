---
title: "Your own token images, from a folder on your machine"
date: 2026-11-08
area: ui
issues: [27]
---
- Put your token images in the page's local tokens folder (one PNG or WebP per role, named like `imp.webp` or `fortune-teller.webp`) and reload: they replace the drawn tokens in the square and the grimoire.
- Any role without an image keeps its drawn token, and nothing flickers while the page looks.
- A shroud image, a ghost vote image and reminder images work the same way, if you want them.
- The game bar says "local art: N of 22 tokens" when it found any.
- That folder is never committed: only its short instructions are, so the public repo still carries none of the official art.
