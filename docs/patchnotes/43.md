---
title: "The page follows the game live, at a pace you can read"
date: 2026-11-07
area: ui
issues: [26]
---
- The page now shows each moment of the game as it happens: lines appear as they are said, and in a vote you see each hand go up or stay down as it is cast.
- If the connection to the relay drops, the page catches up when it reconnects and shows nothing twice.
- A new "pace" choice on the top bar: fast, readable or slow. It starts on readable, about a minute between one player's turns, so a day is slow enough to follow.
- The pace applies to players you start after choosing it. A small note says when players already running are still on the old pace.
