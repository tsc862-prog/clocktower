---
title: Rooms for small talks, whispers only between neighbours
date: 2026-11-08
area: rules
issues: [28]
---
- The day now has three rooms besides the Square, for two, three and four players; a player can go to one or take someone along, and come back when they like.
- Everyone sees who went where, but only the people in a room hear what is said there, and someone who walks in late hears nothing said before they arrived.
- At the Square a player may whisper only to the nearest living player on either side; the table still sees the whisper happen. There are no whispers in a room.
- A line in a room counts as that player's turn of the round, and when nominations open the rooms close and everyone comes back to the Square.
- Dead players may use the rooms and talk there too.
- The seeded practice game now uses the rooms, so its printout shows who went where each day; who wins does not change. The players' own buttons for rooms come next.
