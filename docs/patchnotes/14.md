---
title: "The players get their tools"
date: 2026-11-07
area: players
issues: [1]
---
- Nothing you will see in a game yet: each player now has what it needs to play its seat, ready for when the agents sit down.
- A player can look at the table, read its own role and what the Storyteller told it, catch up on the talk, keep private notes, speak, whisper, nominate, vote and make its night choice.
- At the start of every turn a player sees a short card: who it is, the time of day, who is alive, what is new, and in bold what is waiting on it.
- A player only ever learns what its character could: never another seat's role, never a whisper between two others, never the Storyteller's reasons.
- A player who repeats itself is told to say something new or pass.
