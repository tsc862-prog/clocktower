---
title: "The narrator voice switches itself on when the speech engine is running"
date: 2026-11-08
area: infra
issues: [50]
---
- When you start the game, it now looks for the speech engine on your PC by itself; if it finds it, the launcher says "voice engine found" with the number of voices, and the narrator can speak with no setting to change.
- If no engine answers, the launcher says so and the game stays silent, as before. You can still force it off or on in your settings file.
- The relay now also hands the page the voice chosen for each seat, once that list exists.
