date: 2026-11-08

- Once portraits of the seats have been made and accepted, each seat in the square shows its portrait inside its disc instead of its initial.
- A dead seat keeps the shroud over its portrait, which turns grey.
- The agents panel shows a small portrait beside each seat and the Storyteller, and the Storyteller's log shows the Storyteller's.
- Until a portrait exists, or if one cannot be read, the seat keeps its initial and nothing else changes.
