---
title: Game 1 fixes for the players
date: 2026-11-07
area: players
issues: [33, 31, 32, 38, 29]
---
- Each line at the table shows the speaker's name once: no more "Corwin: Corwin: ...".
- The first day should have more to read: the players are told to say something about themselves or ask someone a question rather than pass, and to nominate someone when nobody stands out instead of letting the day end with no vote.
- Each of the seven neighbours now has a manner of their own (methodical Odette, blunt Bram, curious Ines, cautious Corwin, warm Thea, dry Fitch, suspicious Yusuf), so the table should stop repeating one phrase seven times.
- With four or fewer alive, every player is reminded what a wrong execution costs: how many are left after today and after tonight, and that evil wins at two.
- A player who has already had their turn this round is told plainly to wait, so fewer turns are spent on moves the game refuses.
