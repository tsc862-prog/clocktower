---
title: Evil players keep their roles secret
date: 2026-11-09
area: players
issues: [104]
---
- An evil player now claims a good role from the first day and keeps to it: the demon picks one of the roles it was told are not in play, the Spy one she saw missing from the grimoire, another minion a quiet role nobody has claimed yet.
- The demon and its minions keep each other's secrets: when one tells the other its real role in a room, the other is reminded never to repeat it, so you should no longer hear an evil player hand a team-mate to the table.
- Minions are nudged to agree their story with the demon in a room, so the two of them do not claim the same role.
- When someone claims to be the Chef, the table is reminded that the number counts evil pairs anywhere around the table, not the Chef's own neighbours.
