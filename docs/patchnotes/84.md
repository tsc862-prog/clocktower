---
title: "Players know there is only one of each character"
date: 2026-11-09
area: players
issues: [81]
---
- Players are now told plainly that each character is in play at most once: two people claiming the Mayor means at most one of them is the Mayor, and the other is lying or is the Drunk. You should hear "you can't both be the Mayor" instead of "you are the only Mayor left".
- When two players have claimed the same role out loud, each player's turn now says so in its list of claims, so nobody has to count to notice the clash.
- The Storyteller's instructions carry the same rule.
