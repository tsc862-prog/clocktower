---
title: "The three rooms get their Thistlewick names"
date: 2026-11-08
area: lore
issues: [28]
---
- The places where a few neighbours can talk out of the table's hearing now have names of the town's own: the Snug (room for two), the Vestry (three) and the Taproom (four).
- The story, the page and the transcripts say "Bram and Ines went to the Snug" instead of the plain placeholders.
