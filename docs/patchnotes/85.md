date: 2026-11-09

- With Voice on, the Storyteller now also says when night falls, when a round begins, when nominations open and when the day ends.
- Every move between rooms is spoken too: who went to which room, and who came back to the Square.
- Before each seat speaks, a short name is said in the Storyteller's voice: "Odette." at the Square, "Odette, in the Snug." in a room, and "Odette, to Bram." for a whisper when whispers are ticked.
- A new "names" tick next to "whispers" turns the name tags off. It is on by default and the page remembers your choice.
- Vote results are now read out as well.
