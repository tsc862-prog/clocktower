- Nothing you will notice while watching: the relay now keeps a record of what happened at the table (nights, dawns, talk, passes, whispers, moves, nominations, votes, executions, deaths and the win), once each, for the report.
- The behavior report names the winner and counts the days, lines and passes from that record, so a game whose Storyteller never narrated the end no longer reads "winner not in the logs". Older games read as before.
