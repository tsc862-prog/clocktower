---
title: "The page keeps pace with the voices"
date: 2026-11-09
area: ui
issues: [103]
---
- With Voice on, the page now shows only what the voices have said: when the Storyteller says someone goes to the Snug, the rooms break out as you hear it, not before.
- A vote's hands still rise as they are cast, but the tally and the flash on the nominee land as the result is read aloud.
- Dawn's deaths, nightfall and the talk itself wait for their reading too, so the screen never runs ahead of your ears.
- While the page is waiting for the voices, the voice bar says "the page keeps pace"; Mute or Voice off shows the live game at once.
