title: "A whole game plays itself, start to finish"
date: 2026-11-07
area: rules
issues: [1]

- A full game of Trouble Brewing now plays from the deal to a winner with seven stand-in players, no AI yet: nights, deaths at dawn, nominations, votes and executions.
- Every role works as the game has it, including the tricky ones: the Drunk who thinks they are someone else, poison, the Spy and the Recluse showing up as something they are not, the Scarlet Woman taking over, the Virgin, the Slayer, the Mayor and the Saint.
- The same game number always plays out the same way, so any odd moment can be replayed exactly.
- Nothing to watch on the page yet: this is the rulebook the Storyteller and the players will be held to.
