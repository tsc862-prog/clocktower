- The square is now a round table seen from above, with the seven players sitting round it in order. Each player's name runs round the rim of their seat marker.
- Every role has its own round token with a simple picture and its name. The colour of the rim tells the team: blue townsfolk, teal outsiders, orange minions, red the demon.
- The dead get a shroud over their seat, and their ghost vote is a small coin: gold while they still have it, faded once it is spent.
- In a vote you see each hand go up or stay down, with the nominee ringed in red and the nominator in gold.
- The grimoire shows each role as its token and the reminders (poisoned, drunk, protected, master, red herring, dead) as small discs beside the player.
- The drawings are all ours and easy to change, so tell us if you would like another style.
