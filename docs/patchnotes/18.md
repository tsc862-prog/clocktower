- Nothing you will notice: every change is now checked for leaks between seats. A secret is planted in one player (their role, their night information, a note, a whisper) and every other seat's tools, header and prompt are read for it; none may carry it.
- The same check proves the public lines reach everyone, the living and the dead, and that a whisper reaches exactly its two players.
- It also plays a whole seeded game through the relay with stand-in players and gets the same winner the headless game does.
