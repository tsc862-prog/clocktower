---
title: "The Storyteller tells each death as it happened and closes the game"
date: 2026-11-09
area: storyteller
issues: [90, 91]
---
- When a player dies at once for nominating someone, the Storyteller now says the nomination killed them on the spot. It can no longer say the town voted them off.
- A shot fired by day is now narrated too: who fired at whom and what came of it, even when nothing happened.
- When the game ends, the Storyteller narrates it before stopping: what happened last (a death in the final night included), who won and how, and who was who.
- A game resumed with the scripted Storyteller still hears every dawn, execution and the end, told in plain facts.
- Where the demon's kill on the Mayor lands is now plainly the Storyteller's choice, and its reason has to say what it weighed.
- The Storyteller is no longer woken during the day when nothing waits on it, so its turns in the agents panel and the logs are real calls.
